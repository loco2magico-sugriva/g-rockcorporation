History of the G-ROCK Parliament

The Parliament of the Monkeys from Gibraltar was established in 1943, following the takeover of Gibraltar Rock by the Ape population.

The first Constitution laid the foundation for direct self-governance and independence.

The December 1963 amendment formally incorporated the strategic leaders — Chairman Professor Sugriva and Corporal Vojtek.

G-ROCK Constitution

Principles of Direct Democracy

Every member of the population has the right to vote and to propose legislation. The rights of the Apes form the foundation of Gibraltar Rock's independence.

Every Ape has the right to vote, to propose legislative frameworks, and to directly shape constitutional decisions.

December 1963 Amendment (Veto Clause)

Corporal Vojtek was granted the Absolute Right of Veto. The amendment was passed because the Ape population placed unconditional trust in him and wished to guarantee that Vojtek would never feel discriminated against or excluded within Ape society as the only bear in the entire structure.
