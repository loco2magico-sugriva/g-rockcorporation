Context

G-Rock is a universe built on real events from 2024–2025, from a period of study at British university. Based on these experiences, the author created a world combining authentic stories of remarkable animals — such as Corporal Wojtek or the Gibraltar macaques — with the character of Professor Sugriva.

Academic Absurdity

This section covers an academic dispute from 2024–2025, triggered by a failed interview — rejected for the bizarre reason that the conversation was mostly about the fact of having written a book — followed by an unlawful demand to verify country of origin for the purposes of an academic essay.

Professor Sugriva and Monkeys University of Gibraltar

In response to the demand to verify identity and origin (by passport or other proof), the author attributed authorship of his own quotes to Sugriva — a real chimpanzee — thereby giving rise to a fictional university.

Confrontation with the Facts

An attempt to explain the "professor's" real identity to a university staff member (Jessica). The student wrote in an email: "Do you remember Professor Sugriva from Monkeys University of Gibraltar?", then attached links to Guardian articles and safari footage, clearly showing that Sugriva is in fact a chimpanzee, and that the whole structure was a response to the unlawful requirements imposed earlier.

05
🏅

Academic Recognition of Fiction

A university representative, in an official email, described the quote attributed to Professor Sugriva as intriguing and thought-provoking on freedom and resilience. The message praised his speech as captivating, emphasizing the profound impact of Professor Sugriva's work and ideas on the student.

Escalation

After the chimpanzee was accepted as a professor, the student decided to end his relationship with the university and sent a presentation on AI-related risks, attributing the prototypes it contained to Professor Sugriva. The material presented concepts of his authorship — including a system that detected the user's reluctance to go to work, which automatically generated and sent, from his phone, a message to the employer giving a reason for absence.
